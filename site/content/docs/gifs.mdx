title: GIFs
description: Search, send and insert GIFs from the composer with your own GIPHY or KLIPY key.
---

## Open the picker

The GIF button in the composer's action bar opens the picker. It appears only
when at least one GIF provider is enabled.

- `cmd-shift-g` on macOS, `ctrl-shift-g` on Windows and Linux.
- Type `/gif` and a search, for example `/gif cats`. The command never leaves
  your machine: it opens the picker with the search box already filled in.

## Your key, your provider

Parla offers two providers, **GIPHY** and **KLIPY**, but it has no API key of
its own: nothing is sent to either service until you enable one with your own
key. A provider you have not set up shows a set-up card in its tab:

1. Open the key page of the provider: [GIPHY](https://developers.giphy.com/dashboard)
   or [KLIPY](https://partner.klipy.com).
2. Create an app and copy its API key.
3. Paste the key into the **API key** field and choose **Enable**.

The key is stored in the system keychain of your computer, not in Parla's
settings file and not in its logs. The card lists the hosts the provider talks
to. Settings, `gifs.providers` chooses which providers the picker offers.

## What is sent

Only after you enable a provider, and only for the tab you are using, Parla
sends the text in the search box (or nothing, when the tab shows trending) and
your content rating to that provider's servers. The rating is `gifs.rating`,
`pg-13` by default. The provider returns the results; Parla keeps no copy of
them beyond the list the open picker shows.

## Send or insert

- **Enter** sends the focused GIF as a message of its own. A draft you were
  writing stays in the composer.
- **Shift-Enter** inserts the GIF's link into the draft at the cursor, with a
  space where the text needs one.
- Click a GIF to send it. The arrow keys move in the grid, Up from the first
  row returns to the search box, Tab switches provider tabs and Escape closes
  the picker.

`gifs.send_as` chooses what sending does. With **link** the message is the
GIF's URL, which services show inline. With **upload** Parla downloads the GIF
and uploads it as a file, which also works where links do not unfurl.
`gifs.autoplay_in_messages` decides whether GIFs in messages play always, when
hovered or never.
