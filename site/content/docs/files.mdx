## Previews

Files shared in a conversation appear as cards under the message:

- **Images** show inline, fitted to a box.
- **PDFs** show the thumbnail the service supplies. Parla does not render PDF
  pages itself.
- **Snippets** show the file name, language, line numbers and the first lines.
- **Clips** are video or audio files with a poster, name, size and duration.
- **Other files** show an icon, name and size.
- Deleted files show a placeholder.

The **Inline images** setting in Settings, General controls when images load:
**Always**, **On click** or **Never**. The default is **On click**. Images over
10 MB are not loaded inline, and animated images play only while visible.

## Open a file

Click an image to open it in the media viewer. The viewer shows the file name,
who sent it, when and its size.

- Use **Previous** and **Next**, the Left and Right arrow keys, or the
  thumbnails to step through the loaded images of the conversation.
- Zoom with `+` and `-`, use `0` for actual size and `F` to fit the window.
  Drag or scroll to pan when the image is larger than the window.
- **Copy image**, **Download**, **Open in default app** and **Open in channel**
  are in the toolbar. Escape closes the viewer.

Click a code or text file, or **Open in viewer** on a snippet card, to open the
code viewer. It shows the file name, who sent it, where and when, with line
numbers, wrapping, copy and download. `cmd-f` opens find inside the file;
Escape closes find, and a second Escape closes the window.

Snippets show a preview of at most 400 lines or 64 KB. When the preview is only
part of the file, the card says so, **Copy preview** copies only the visible
lines, and **Download** saves the whole file.

## Clips

On macOS, **Play** downloads the clip into the cache and plays it with the
system decoder. Parla never starts a clip by itself. Click the card again to
pause and resume without taking the keyboard away from the composer.

The card shows pause and resume, a seek bar, speed (1x, 1.5x and 2x), mute and
volume, and captions when the service supplies them. With the card focused,
Space plays or pauses, Left and Right move 5 seconds, and `m` mutes. A clip
pauses when it scrolls out of view or the window loses focus.

On Windows and Linux, and on macOS when playback is not possible, the card shows
the poster and **Open in browser**.

## Downloads

**Download** saves the file to your downloads folder under a unique name, so
nothing is overwritten. A toast confirms the save and offers **Show in folder**.

**Open in default app** saves the file first, then opens only PNG, JPEG, GIF,
WebP, HEIC and PDF files whose name and type agree. Any other file is only
saved. Downloaded files carry the system's mark for files from the internet, so
the operating system asks before opening or running them.

## Uploads

Attach files in three ways:

- Drag files from your file manager onto the timeline.
- Paste files or an image from the clipboard into the composer with `cmd-v`
  (`ctrl-v` on Linux and Windows).
- Use the **Attach files** button in the composer.

Files do not upload until you press **Send**. They sit in the composer as chips
with a thumbnail or type badge, the name and the size, and each has a remove
button. Your text becomes the comment on the files and may be empty. The thread
composer works the same way.

When you send, each chip shows **Uploading** with its size and a **Cancel**
button. **Send** waits for the uploads to finish. If an upload fails, the chip
turns red with the reason and offers **Retry** and **Remove**. Uploads need a
connection and are not queued: sending while offline shows an error and keeps
the files attached.

Files larger than 1 GB are refused, and directories are refused too. Other
files in the same drop still upload.

## Long pastes become snippets

Pasting more than 20 lines or more than 2,000 characters into the composer does
not fill the draft. Parla offers to share the text as a snippet instead: give it
a title, check the language it guessed, and choose **Share as snippet**. The
text is attached as a file chip and sent like any other file.

Choose **Paste as text** or press Escape to put the text in the draft instead. A
paste over 1 MB is never a snippet; Parla offers to attach it as an ordinary
text file.

## The Files tab

The **Files** tab under a conversation header lists the files shared in that
conversation, newest first, including thread replies. Each row shows the file
type, name, size, who shared it and when.

Click a row, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space, to open it:
images and PDFs in the media viewer, snippets and code files in the code viewer,
and other files as downloads. The list loads in pages as you scroll, up to 500
files. **Messages** goes back to the conversation.

The tab lists the files of messages Parla has stored, so older files appear once
their messages have been loaded.
