Parla's main window has four parts: the workspace rail on the left, the sidebar
with your conversations, the timeline in the middle and a right panel for
threads, search, profiles and details.

The keys below are the macOS defaults. On Linux and Windows, use `ctrl` in
place of `cmd`. Every shortcut can be changed in [Settings](/parla/docs/settings/)
or `keymap.json`; see [Keyboard shortcuts](/parla/docs/keymap/).

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists your conversations in these sections:

- **Unreads**, when you turn it on. It lists every unread conversation.
- **Starred**, when you have any. Stars are Parla's own list for this computer.
- **Your own sections**, above Channels, in the order you put them.
- **Channels** you are a member of, by name.
- **Direct messages**, with the most recent activity first.
- **Threads**, **Later**, **Scheduled** and **Reminders**, when supported, each
  with its icon and a count at the end.
- **Drafts**, while you have unsaved text.
- **People**, to open the workspace directory.

Channels carry a hash, private channels a lock, and people show their avatar
with a presence dot. An unread conversation is bold. Click a section title, or
press Enter or Space on it, to fold it. Parla remembers folded sections per
workspace. The quick links at the top are tab stops too: press Enter or Space on
one to open it.

Right-click a conversation for its menu: view details, star it, change its
notification level, mute it, mark it read, move it to one of your sections or
open it in a new window. Channel admins can also edit, add people, leave or
archive a channel there.

A channel's member list and member count update live as people join or leave it,
and a channel created or joined from another client appears without a reload.

The **+** after Channels opens **Browse channels**, where you can search, join
or create a channel. The **+** after Direct messages lists people.

To move conversations into your own sections, choose **Organise sidebar** from
the workspace menu. Drag conversations onto a section, or drag sections to
reorder them, then choose **Done**. These sections stay on this computer and are
not sent to the service.

The workspace menu at the top of the sidebar opens Settings, changes your status
or presence, pauses notifications, switches workspace, signs out or removes the
workspace.

## Read and catch up

Opening a conversation moves its read marker forward. Days are separated by a
divider, and a **New** divider marks the first unread message when you open a
conversation with unreads.

To mark a message unread, select it and press `cmd-shift-u`, right-click it and
choose **Mark unread**, or alt-click it. The read marker moves back to that
message.

Three views at the top of the workspace rail cover every workspace:

- **All unreads** (`cmd-shift-a`).
- **All mentions** (no default key; bind `workspace::AllMentions`).
- **All scheduled** (no default key; bind `workspace::AllScheduled`).

All unreads lists unread conversations, mentions first. You can mark one
conversation or all of them read from there. Looking at the view itself marks
nothing read.

Messages from apps and bots carry an **APP** tag after the author's name.

## Write and send

Click the composer and type. By default, Enter sends and Shift-Enter makes a new
line. With **Send key** set to Command-Enter in Settings, Appearance, Enter
makes a new line and `cmd-enter` sends.

| Key           | Action                                        |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| `enter`       | Send                                          |
| `shift-enter` | New line                                      |
| `up`          | Edit your last message                        |
| `escape`      | Cancel an edit or a reply                     |
| `tab`         | Insert the selected mention, channel or emoji |

To edit your own message, select it and press `e`. To delete it, press
`cmd-backspace`. Parla asks before deleting. Edit and delete only work on
messages you are allowed to change.

Parla keeps the text in a composer as a draft for each conversation and thread.
Drafts are saved when you switch conversation, close a thread or quit. Clearing
the composer deletes the draft. **Drafts** in the sidebar lists every draft,
newest first.

When someone else is typing, a line above the composer says who. Your own typing
never shows.

## Mentions, emoji and formatting

Type `@` to pick a person or user group, or `@here`, `@channel` or `@everyone`
outside direct messages. Type `#` to pick a channel, or `:` and two letters to
pick an emoji. Use the arrow keys to move, Enter or Tab to insert, and Escape to
close the list.

A picked mention or channel is one unit: Backspace deletes all of it, and it is
sent as a real mention. A name you only type, such as `@ana` without picking it,
is sent as plain text. Mentions are drawn as tinted chips, whether or not they
name you.

The row above the composer formats the selection, or inserts an empty pair when
nothing is selected. Press the same shortcut again to remove the markers.

| Shortcut          | Format        | Writes                |
| ----------------- | ------------- | --------------------- |
| `cmd-b`           | Bold          | `*text*`              |
| `cmd-i`           | Italic        | `_text_`              |
| `cmd-shift-x`     | Strikethrough | `~text~`              |
| `cmd-e`           | Code          | `` `text` ``          |
| `cmd-alt-shift-c` | Code block    | three backticks       |
| `cmd-shift-9`     | Quote         | `> ` before a line    |
| `cmd-shift-8`     | Bulleted list | `• ` before a line    |
| `cmd-shift-7`     | Numbered list | `1. ` before a line   |
| `cmd-shift-u`     | Link          | a web or mail address |

The smiley button opens the emoji picker. It has fuzzy search, a keyboard grid,
skin tones and a **Recent** row. Custom emoji from the workspace show their image
in the `:` suggestions and in the picker: the **Custom** tab lists all of them
alphabetically, and search finds both standard and custom emoji. A custom emoji
also shows as its image in the message box, and typing the whole `:name:` turns
it into the emoji, the same as picking it.

Type `/` at the start of an empty composer to list commands. Parla supports
`/remind`, `/dnd`, `/status`, `/away` and `/active` when the service does, plus
`/shrug`. A command Parla cannot run shows "/name is not supported in Parla"
with a link to open the conversation in the service's own client.

## Reactions and threads

Reactions appear as pills under a message. Click a pill to add or remove your
own reaction, and hover it to see who reacted. Press `cmd-shift-\` on a selected
message to open the emoji picker and add a reaction.

A message with replies shows a summary under it: the reply authors' avatars, the
reply count and when the last reply arrived. Click it, or press `cmd-shift-t` on
the message, to open the thread in the right panel. While the thread is open, the
root message is highlighted and the summary reads **Open in panel**. Press
Escape to close the panel.

The thread composer sends replies to the thread. The thread header has a bell:
a highlighted bell means you follow the thread and get its new replies. You
follow a thread you replied in. A reply can also be sent to the main
conversation; bind `composer::ToggleBroadcast` if you want a key for that.

## Search

Press `cmd-shift-f` to open search in the right panel. From a conversation the
field starts with `in:#name`, so the search stays there; delete that to search
everywhere.

Type to search. Messages stored on this device appear first. When the service
can search on its server, its results follow under a **From** label. Without a
connection, Parla searches only the device and says so.

Filters work as words in the field:

- `in:#channel` and `from:@name`.
- `before:` and `after:` with a date such as `2026-11-01`.
- `has:file`, `has:link`, `has:reaction` or `has:pin`.

Use the arrow keys to move through results and Enter to open one. A message
opens its conversation scrolled to it. A person opens their profile. Escape
closes the panel.

## Send later, scheduled messages and reminders

Choose **Send later** under the composer, or press `cmd-shift-enter`, to send
the text at a later time. The menu lists your presets, by default tomorrow 09:00
and Monday 09:00, with their date and time. **Custom...** accepts a date
(`YYYY-MM-DD`) and a 24-hour time (`HH:MM`). Times use your profile's time zone,
or `send_later.timezone` when you set one. A time in the past, or more than 120
days ahead, cannot be chosen.

If the service can schedule messages, it sends them even when Parla is closed.
Otherwise the message is kept on this device and Parla must be running.
**Scheduled** in the sidebar lists every scheduled message of the workspace.
Server items can be edited, sent now or deleted. Local items are tagged "local,
needs app open". Failed items show why, with Retry and Discard.

**Reminders** in the sidebar lists overdue, upcoming, recurring and completed
reminders. Write what to remember and when, then press Enter or **Add
reminder**. Times use words such as `in 20 minutes`, `at 15:30`,
`tomorrow at 09:00`, `on monday at 09:00`, `on 2026-12-01` or `every monday`.

When the service keeps reminders, Parla asks it to add, complete and delete
them. Otherwise Parla keeps them on this computer and alerts you with a desktop
notification when one is due, as long as Parla is running. You can snooze,
complete, edit or delete a reminder from the list.

## People, profiles and status

**People** opens the workspace directory. Each card shows a person's picture,
name, title, presence and local time when the service shares them. Type to
search by name or title, and use the filter chips for your time zone or bots.

Click a person's name or a mention to open their profile in the right panel.
Their profile shows their status, local time, presence and contact fields, with
a **Message** button that opens your direct message with them.

Your own profile is at the bottom of the workspace rail. **Set a status...** in
the workspace menu opens the status dialog. Pick an emoji, write the text and
choose when it clears: never, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, today, this week or a
custom time. Presets such as Focusing, In a meeting, Commuting, Out sick and On
holiday fill all three at once. **Set yourself as away** and **Set yourself as
active** switch your presence.

Presence appears as a small dot: filled when someone is active, a ring when they
are away. Parla asks about presence only for the people on screen.

## Move around

- **Quick switcher:** `cmd-k`. Type to filter channels, direct messages, groups
  and people in every workspace. Enter opens the selected item and Escape
  closes.
- **Back and forward:** `cmd-[` and `cmd-]`.
- **Command palette:** `cmd-shift-p`. It lists the actions you can run, with
  their keys.
- **Focus next pane:** `cmd-f6`; previous pane: `cmd-shift-f6`.
- **Open in a new window:** right-click a conversation, use the thread panel's
  **Open in new window**, or press `cmd-shift-o` with a conversation or thread
  in focus.
- **Jump to the latest message:** `cmd-shift-down`.
- **Shortcuts sheet:** `cmd-/`.

On macOS the titlebar has back and forward arrows, a search control with its
search icon and `cmd-shift-f` hint, a help button that opens this documentation, and a
bell that opens the pause-notifications menu for the current workspace. While
notifications are paused the bell is crossed out.

## Huddles and calls

When a huddle is live in a conversation, its sidebar row shows headphones in
presence green and the number of people in it. A bar above the timeline lists up to five people,
how long ago the huddle started, and **Join**.

By default Parla joins with its own audio: the call bar shows **Connecting...**,
then the number of people, with **Mute** and **Leave**. If the microphone is
unavailable you can still listen. If native audio cannot connect, Parla tries
the call window on macOS, then the browser, then the service's own app.

**Devices** in the call bar picks the microphone and speaker, or the system
default. The change applies to the running call at once. Video and screen
sharing are not carried yet; when someone starts sharing, the bar offers **Open
in browser**.

When someone rings you in a direct-message huddle, Parla shows a notification
with **Join** and **Decline**, a banner with the same buttons and a chime. The
ring stops when the caller hangs up, when the huddle ends, when you answer
elsewhere, or after 45 seconds; a missed ring becomes a "Missed huddle"
notification.

You can turn ringing off, choose the ringtone, choose how Parla joins and mute
the microphone on join in Settings, Calls. A ring never sounds while
notifications are paused, during quiet hours, when desktop notifications are
off, or when ringing is turned off.
